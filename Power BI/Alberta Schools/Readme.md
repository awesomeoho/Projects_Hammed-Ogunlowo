# Alberta School Selection Analytics

## Project Overview

The Alberta economy experienced a recession that significantly affected the construction of new schools planned for the year. In response, the Government of Alberta decided to maximize the use of existing schools and facilities.

This Power BI dashboard provides parents and guardians with accessible information about schools across Alberta. It is designed to support school selection by making it easier to compare location, education level, school type, and study options.

## Key Questions Answered

The dashboard presents five parent-focused visual stories:

1. **How many schools are available?**
	- The overview reports approximately **9.5K schools** across Alberta.
	- It also summarizes the education levels served: Early Childhood Services (**5.5K**), Elementary Education (**6.4K**), Junior High Education (**4.7K**), and Senior High Education (**3K**).
	- These figures help families understand the breadth of school coverage and identify the education stages available in the province.

2. **Where are schools located?**
	- The map shows the geographic distribution of schools across Alberta.
	- School locations are concentrated around major population centres, while additional schools serve smaller and more rural communities.
	- Parents can use the **School City** slicer to focus the analysis on a specific city and compare local availability.

3. **What types of schools are available?**
	- Public schools are the dominant school type, with approximately **6.0K** schools.
	- Separate schools follow with approximately **1.6K**, while private schools account for approximately **0.7K**.
	- The dashboard also shows smaller categories, including ECS private operators, provincial, federal Indian affairs, Francophone, charter, and federal jail schools.
	- This gives parents a starting point for understanding the range of school governance and operating models available in Alberta.

4. **What study types are offered?**
	- The study-type chart compares **Blended**, **Home Education**, **Online**, and **Outreach** options.
	- The similar bar heights indicate that these alternatives are available at meaningful scale across the dataset.
	- Families seeking flexibility can use this view to identify whether a city offers options beyond a traditional in-person arrangement.

5. **Which education stage best matches the child’s needs?**
	- The education-level indicators allow parents to distinguish schools serving early childhood, elementary, junior high, and senior high students.
	- The larger elementary and early-childhood totals suggest broad coverage for younger learners, while the lower senior-high total highlights the importance of checking local availability when planning later-stage education.

## Main Insights

- Alberta has a large and geographically distributed school network that can be explored by city.
- Public schools make up the largest share of the available school landscape.
- Parents have access to multiple school types and study formats, including online, blended, home education, and outreach options.
- Availability changes by location and education stage, so a provincial total should be used as a starting point rather than a substitute for city-level research.
- The dashboard can help narrow a shortlist, but families should confirm admissions requirements, transportation, program details, and current capacity with the school or school authority.
